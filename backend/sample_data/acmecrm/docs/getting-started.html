What is AcmeCRM?

AcmeCRM is our CRM platform used by the sales team to track contacts, deals, and pipeline activity. If you're new, start with the account setup checklist below.

Quick Setup Checklist

  • Confirm your workspace plan (Starter, Professional, or Enterprise) with your manager before importing large contact lists, since Starter caps at 1,000 contacts.
  • Import contacts via Contacts → Import using a CSV or Excel file.
  • Connect your email under Settings → Email Sync.
  • If your team uses the API or webhooks, confirm you're on the Professional plan or higher — this is not available on Starter.

Where to get help

Support response times depend on plan: 48 hours on Starter, 24 hours on Professional (priority), and a 4-hour SLA on Enterprise (dedicated contact).
