# AcmeCRM Product Overview

AcmeCRM is a customer relationship management platform for small and mid-sized sales teams. It centralizes contacts, deals, email tracking, and reporting in one workspace.

## Core Modules

- **Contacts** — a searchable database of people and companies, with custom fields, tags, and activity timelines.
- **Deals** — a pipeline view (kanban-style) for tracking opportunities through configurable stages.
- **Email Sync** — two-way sync with Gmail and Outlook; logs sent/received emails against the matching contact automatically.
- **Reporting** — dashboards for pipeline value, conversion rate by stage, and rep activity.
- **Automations** — rule-based workflows (e.g. "when a deal moves to Closed Won, send a Slack notification").
- **Webhooks & API** — programmatic access to contacts, deals, and events for custom integrations (Professional plan and above — see api-reference.md).

## Who uses AcmeCRM

Primarily B2B sales teams of 3–200 people who've outgrown a spreadsheet but don't need the complexity of a full enterprise CRM suite.
