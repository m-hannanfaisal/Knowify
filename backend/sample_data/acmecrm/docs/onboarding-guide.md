# AcmeCRM Onboarding Guide

Welcome to AcmeCRM. This guide walks a new workspace through first setup.

## Step 1: Create Your Workspace

After signing up, you'll be prompted to name your workspace and choose your plan (Starter, Professional, or Enterprise — see `product-overview.md` for a comparison). You can change plans at any time from **Settings → Billing**.

## Step 2: Import Your Contacts

Go to **Contacts → Import**. AcmeCRM accepts CSV or Excel files. Map your columns to AcmeCRM fields (Name, Email, Company, etc.) on the next screen. Imports on the Starter plan are capped at 1,000 contacts total, matching the plan's contact limit.

## Step 3: Invite Your Team

Under **Settings → Team**, invite teammates by email. Starter includes 3 users, Professional includes 10, and Enterprise is unlimited — see `feature-comparison.xlsx` for exact seat pricing beyond the included count.

## Step 4: Connect Your Email

Go to **Settings → Email Sync** and connect Gmail or Outlook. This enables automatic logging of sent/received emails against matching contacts. This step is optional but strongly recommended.

## Step 5 (Professional/Enterprise only): Set Up API Access

If you're on Professional or Enterprise, go to **Settings → API Keys** to generate a key for any custom integrations, and **Settings → Webhooks** to register endpoints for real-time events. Full details are in `api-reference.md`.

## Step 6: Customize Your Pipeline

Under **Deals → Pipeline Settings**, rename or reorder deal stages to match your sales process. The default pipeline is: New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won / Closed Lost.

## Getting Help

- Starter plan: email support@acmecrm.com, 48-hour response target
- Professional: priority support, 24-hour response target
- Enterprise: dedicated support contact with a 4-hour SLA
